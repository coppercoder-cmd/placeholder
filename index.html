01 · The problem

You've outgrown the spreadsheet.

Ask five people at a growing company who owns onboarding, and you'll get five different answers. Somewhere between "a founder and a laptop" and "enterprise IT," the handover between HR and IT becomes nobody's job in particular.

Where it breaks

A new hire waits until lunch for system access. An offboarding checklist lives in one person's head. Nobody notices an ex-employee still has access until it matters.

How Tarid closes it

One workflow HR and IT both work from. Every hire triggers the right access automatically. Every departure revokes it the same day — logged, timestamped, done.

02 · The product

Built for security, clarity and operational confidence

Onboarding

New hires get the right tools and access from hour one — no chasing IT, no waiting on HR, no guesswork about who approves what.

Offboarding

Access is revoked the moment someone leaves. No forgotten logins, no orphaned accounts, no exposure you find out about too late.

Audit & Compliance

Every action is tracked and timestamped, so you can answer a customer's security questionnaire in minutes, not days.
